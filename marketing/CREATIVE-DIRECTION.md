# Dewhaven — A quiet life. A hidden adventure.

English-language store campaign for the native Dewhaven 2.4 game, inspected on 23 October 2026. The newer Unity edition is the source of truth; the older browser game is not used as gameplay evidence.

## Visual direction

Forest teal, antique brass, warm ivory and terracotta. A welcoming inherited farmhouse and a small key connect the farming and exploration themes. The icon contains no people. Key art contains no people. Gameplay uses the farmer's rear view; no full-face portraits or dialogue portrait panels are used.

The existing game has painted opening scenes and a pixel-art playable world. Promotional illustration is used for the icon, banner and short video end card. Store screenshots and the main video scenes show native rendered gameplay from an isolated demonstration save. Staging does not establish completion of a natural playthrough.

## Deliverables

- Play Store icon: 512 × 512 RGBA PNG, under 1 MB; higher-resolution original retained.
- Feature graphic: 1024 × 500 RGB PNG. A 2048 × 1000 master and 1920 × 1080 banner are also provided.
- Eight ordered English phone screenshots: 1080 × 1920 RGB PNG, full gameplay below a compact headline band. No device mockups, price badges, store ranking claims or install calls to action.
- Thirty-second Remotion trailer: 1920 × 1080 and 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, H.264/AAC MP4. Both formats are deliberately composed, with the same story and pacing.
- Editable, self-contained Remotion project, source artwork, original captured media, cue sheet, subtitles/accessible transcript, alt text and verification evidence.

## Video structure

0–3 seconds: “Grandma left you a farm.” Show farming immediately.

3–5 seconds: “And a secret.” Cut to the Tide Vault for the opening pattern change.

5–8.5 seconds: “Grow a life.” Farming actions and planted rows.

8.5–12 seconds: “Four towns. Many stories.” Town footage.

12–15 seconds: “One more cast. One deep breath.” Lake fishing.

15–18 seconds: “Small friends. Big feelings.” Animals in the barn.

18–21 seconds: “Follow the paths.” Woodland exploration.

21–24 seconds: “Uncover what was left behind.” Native vault gameplay.

24–27 seconds: “A life worth coming home to.” Homestead.

27–30 seconds: “Dewhaven. A quiet life. A hidden adventure.” Illustrated end card.

Actual game content occupies the first 27 seconds. Each clip and each scene has an individually editable timeline node. On-screen English messaging carries the story without requiring sound. The soundtrack uses the game's original ambient loops with physical foley accents; no stock song is used.
