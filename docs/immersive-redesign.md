# Dewhaven 1.1 — portrait experience

The game world fills the phone. Charcoal, ivory and bronze controls float over the world without a header, greeting, navigation card, location labels or a quest sidebar. Four farming shortcuts, a tool library and the bag sit along the bottom; a left analog stick and right action button remain separate and usable with two fingers. The menu contains the rest of the valley's activities.

Four original generated environment paintings tell a connected opening: Grandmother's letter and key, the journey to Dewhaven, a waiting homestead, and first light in the farmhouse. Two short interactive-guide pages explain walking and the daily loop. All scenes can be skipped or revisited. Narration is written, and audio remains nature ambience and physical effects only.

New stories begin at 6 am outside the farmhouse after a wake-up scene. Plant three seeds for the first chapter, tend the garden and animals, explore and meet neighbors, ship produce, then return home to sleep. Dawn shows the next useful task and overnight earnings. Existing saves retain their progress and do not replay the opening automatically.

Movement has proportional analog speed, gentle acceleration, fast release and collision-aware sliding. Farm and resource changes occur on tool contact, with one mutation and one sound per action. Water has an arcing stream, scattered drops, persistent ripples and responding plants; other tools have distinct soil, seed, wood, stone and harvest effects. Gold changes animate a custom coin and count without covering the play area.

Verification covers cinematic navigation and skipping, save continuity, 320/390/412 portrait layouts, separated touch targets and two-finger input, proportional movement and frame-rate independence, every tool's contact and effects, daily growth and shipping, offline loading, and the packaged Android build. Release output remains a debug APK and an unsigned store bundle.

Version 1.2 keeps this full-screen foundation and makes the joystick floating by default, with configurable handedness/layout/response. It extends the world to 26 scenes, sixteen main chapters, nineteen requests, furnished interiors, three puzzle vaults, relationship moments, weekly town gatherings and generated item art.
