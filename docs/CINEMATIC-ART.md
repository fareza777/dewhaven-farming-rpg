# Dewhaven cinematic artwork

Created with the built-in OpenAI imagegen tool for this project, from text only. No licensed sprite atlases or user files were uploaded. Four distinct portrait paintings were inspected and saved as WebP assets without composition edits. Written narration and interface text are rendered by the game. The original barn/leaf app icon and new tool/coin symbols remain editable SVG graphics.

## Shared prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: full-bleed portrait cinematic illustration for the premium mobile farming game Dewhaven. Generate ONE polished portrait image, approximately 1024 by 1536. Original painterly storybook environment with confident visible brushwork, nuanced watercolor/gouache textures, beautiful architectural shapes and rich natural detail; sophisticated game concept art, not a photo. Consistent palette of warm ivory, antique bronze, amber sunlight, slate blue and softly muted olive accents. Deep atmospheric perspective and cinematic natural light. Important scene details in the central 60% so it crops well to a tall phone screen. Bottom quarter relatively quiet and darker for narrative text added by code. No text, writing, lettering, logos, borders, frames, UI or watermarks. NO people, human figures, faces, animals, birds, insects or living creatures anywhere. Landscape, buildings, plants and still life only.

## Scenes and final saved assets

### letter

Final asset: `public/assets/cinematic/letter.webp`

Scene prompt: Close cinematic still life inside an old timber cottage: a folded blank cream letter and a small antique brass house key on a weathered walnut desk, a ceramic cup, soft warm afternoon light through a window overlooking distant hills. The key and envelope carry the scene in the middle third. Quiet, intimate, a new beginning.

### valley

Final asset: `public/assets/cinematic/valley.webp`

Scene prompt: An elevated winding path leads across a little stone bridge into an uninhabited storybook valley. Amber fields, tiny stone-and-timber village roofs in the distance, pale morning mist, a silver-blue stream and terraced hills. Wide sense of discovery, hopeful sunrise, atmosphere and depth. No vehicles.

### homestead

Final asset: `public/assets/cinematic/homestead.webp`

Scene prompt: A forgotten small timber farmhouse with a warm tiled roof, an open wooden garden gate and fallow garden beds, an old well, wild grasses and modest stone walls. Early morning gold light and low mist over blue distant mountains. A little weathered cottage waiting to become home. Elegant, peaceful, inviting.

### dawn

Final asset: `public/assets/cinematic/dawn.webp`

Scene prompt: View from inside a cozy empty farmhouse bedroom at first light, an open window centered in the upper-middle, overlooking a little farm and distant silver-blue mountains. Cream linen bed at lower-left, plain warm timber floor, work boots and a small watering can by the door, a folded blanket. Sunbeams with delicate dust motes. A quiet first morning and a life ready to begin.
