# Dewhaven — design brief

Build a complete playable 2D farming and village simulation in the current workspace, using the user's local Unity sprite assets. The game has its own name, icon, splash, onboarding, new/continue game, settings, about, share and local rating flows, main and side quests.

## Platform and visual direction
Android phones in portrait orientation are the primary platform. Package the TypeScript/Vite/Canvas game with Capacitor, bundling all assets for offline use from first launch. Provide a branded splash/icon, native Back and background handling, optional vibration, native sharing, and safe save export. The browser version remains a development preview and optional PWA. The supplied source project is Phaser, but only its asset pack is reused. Additional local sprite sheets are extracted from its read-only asset master. Never upload these paid assets to a generation service. Original branding art is generated from text only.

Phone body copy uses 16 px, normal supporting labels 14 px, short decorative captions 13 px, and dense calendar labels 12 px beside 16 px dates. Default tool shortcuts are 48 px and the main action is 88 px, with clear selected states, optional selection/impact vibration, and adjustable size. The two-row tool cluster stays on the action side and mirrors with handedness, keeping the movement corner clear. Large gold values use compact HUD notation with the full amount exposed to accessibility, preventing overlap with time and energy. All item art, including pixel fallbacks, has a transparent background.

Premium charcoal, warm ivory and restrained bronze accents, Fraunces display type and Nunito UI text. Crisp pixel art fills the portrait screen, with floating controls and a thin date/time/energy/gold HUD. No gameplay title, greeting, navigation card, location labels or quest sidebar. Navigation, quest journal and tools live in accessible HTML. Nature ambience and activity SFX are synthesized locally; no instruments or music. Original small sprite faces are permitted in the world and character previews. Four generated cinematic paintings contain scenery, plants and still life without humans or animals.

## Game loop
Create and name a farmer and farm. Four written cinematic scenes connect Grandmother's letter, the journey, the homestead and first light; two short guides explain controls and the daily loop. Start at 6 am outside the house after an illustrated wake-up scene. The first chapter asks for three planted seeds. Tend the farm, explore and meet neighbors, ship produce, then sleep at home. Sleep settles shipping and advances crop growth, weather and a 28-day season. Dawn shows earnings and one useful task. Energy, time, skill levels, friendship and farm reputation persist. The opening can be skipped or replayed without changing saved progress.

Twenty-six playable scenes include four towns, eight outdoor regions, fifteen furnished interiors and three puzzle vaults. A floating joystick starts at the thumb by default; fixed controls, handedness and response are configurable. Tap or hold the action button to work, or tap the world to approach a target. Acceleration, release and camera movement are smoothed while retaining collision sliding. Four farming shortcuts, a tool library and the bag float along the bottom. Menu, journal and pause sit at the upper right. Tools and menu use bottom sheets; catalogs use pagination. Keyboard controls are for development testing. Inventory and journal pause time. All seven tools have physical animation and a single timed impact that changes state. Water arcs, droplets, lingering ripples and plant response accompany watering; soil, seeds, timber, stone and harvesting have distinct effects. Custom coins fly and count on payments. Seasonal grass combines connected meadow patches with smaller animated tufts. Forage, chop and mine renewable resources, play a forgiving fishing minigame, care for animals, cook and craft, meet and gift nine residents.

Sixteen main chapters restore the valley and complete Grandmother's delivery atlas. Each has two written pages; nineteen optional requests have visible requirements and rewards. Three vaults contain persistent mechanisms and unique discoveries. Completion checks consume deliverable items once and issue rewards once. Six adult romance candidates have eighteen written relationship moments, daily conversations, gifts, birthdays, proposals and partner walks. A calendar shows four seasonal festivals and three weekly town gatherings. The core game and story play offline without an account. Android-only AdMob demo ads add menu banners, sparing between-day interstitials and voluntary rewarded energy. A native banner reserves the Android navigation inset plus 8 dp; ads are muted and never cover gameplay. See ADMOB.md.

## Persistence and app shell
Three named local save slots support autosave, explicit save, continue, JSON export/import and corrupt-save recovery. Import validates the schema and migrates original saves without losing progress. Five short settings tabs control movement/layout, sound, display, game pace/language and saves. Display provides wider/closer framing, reduced motion, HUD visibility, larger text and 30/60 fps. Android shares stories and cached save files through its native share sheet. Rating records local feedback until a store listing exists. Android uses packaged assets; the optional browser build has a service worker and web manifest. Thirty-two generated item illustrations share two transparent WebP atlases. Static world details use a three-location background cache and visible-object culling; see EXPANSION.md and ITEM-ART.md.

## Verification
Meaningful unit tests cover crop growth, invalid planting, economy atomicity, shipping, calendar rollover, energy bounds, quest ordering and rewards, inventory capacity, daily social/animal actions, save validation and frame-rate-independent analog movement. Browser tests cover cinematic navigation, morning flow, movement, timed farming impacts, overnight growth/payment, inventory, quests, shops, settings, save/reload and mobile layout. Typecheck and production build must pass; inspect actual screenshots and fix visual and interaction issues. See `immersive-redesign.md` for the version 1.1 scope and `CINEMATIC-ART.md` for artwork provenance.
